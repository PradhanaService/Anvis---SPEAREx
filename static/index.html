SPHEREx Time Viewer

See one patch of sky in every SPHEREx image taken of it, in time order. Data from NASA/IPAC IRSA.

North up, east left. Each frame is the zodiacal-light-subtracted SPHEREx spectral image resampled to a common grid. SPHEREx's wavelength varies across its detectors, so each frame samples a slightly different wavelength (shown above); keep that in mind when comparing brightness between frames. Brightness limits are percentiles of each frame.

Images: SPHEREx spectral images (DR1 and QR3) from the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive, accessed as shown in the SPHEREx tutorial notebooks. Player inspired by WiseView.
